EDITOR > BOOK > COLLECTION > BLOCK > CELL
==========================================

HIERARCHY LEVELS
----------------

Level 0: EDITOR (body > main)
  - Parent: body
  - Children: aside, article
  - Purpose: Viewport, manages open books

Level 1: BOOK (main > article)
  - Parent: main
  - Children: header, section, footer
  - Purpose: Single document with tabs

Level 2: COLLECTION (article > section > section)
  - Parent: article > section (editor area)
  - Children: ul, h2, h1, p
  - Purpose: Layout group (content category)

Level 3: BLOCK (section > ul)
  - Parent: collection (section)
  - Children: li
  - Purpose: Semantic container (list of items)

Level 4: CELL (ul > li)
  - Parent: block (ul)
  - Children: section, article, text
  - Purpose: Leaf content, atomic unit

Level 5: NESTED (li > section)
  - Parent: cell (li)
  - Children: h3, p, ul, table, form, etc.
  - Purpose: Detailed content within cell


RELATIONSHIP RULES
------------------

1. EDITOR contains BOOKS
   - body > main contains article elements
   - Only one book visible at a time (tab switching)

2. BOOK contains HEADER, SECTION, FOOTER
   - article > header: tab navigation only
   - article > section: scrollable editor area
   - article > footer: status bar

3. COLLECTION contains BLOCKS
   - section > section: each collection has ul/ol
   - section > h2: collection title
   - No direct text in collection - must be in blocks

4. BLOCK contains CELLS
   - ul/ol contains li elements
   - No checkbox on content cells
   - Checkbox only on li[aria-checked] (task items)

5. CELLS contain NESTED CONTENT
   - li > section: nested block
   - li > article: nested document
   - li > text: simple content

6. NESTED CONTENT can contain SUB-ITEMS
   - section > ul > li: recursive nesting allowed
   - Maximum depth: 4 levels recommended


SELECTOR PATHS
--------------

Editor:           body > main
Book:             body > main > article
Book Header:      article > header
Editor Area:      article > section
Collection:       article > section > section
Block:            article > section > section > ul
Cell:             article > section > section > ul > li
Nested Section:   ul > li > section
Nested Article:   ul > li > article
Book Footer:      article > footer
Collection Title: article > section > section > h2
Cell Content:     article > section > section > ul > li > section > *


ID NAMING CONVENTION
--------------------

Format: {level}_{book}_{collection}_{block}_{cell}

Examples:
- book_01                          = First book
- book_01_col_01                   = First collection in first book
- book_01_col_01_block_01          = First block in first collection
- book_01_col_01_block_01_cell_01  = First cell in first block
- book_01_col_01_block_01_cell_01_section_01 = Nested section in cell


CSS SPECIFICITY RULES
---------------------

Level 0 (Editor):     body > main
Level 1 (Book):       body > main > article
Level 2 (Collection): body > main > article > section > section
Level 3 (Block):      body > main > article > section > section > ul
Level 4 (Cell):       body > main > article > section > section > ul > li
Level 5 (Nested):     body > main > article > section > section > ul > li > section


SCROLL RULES
------------

Fixed (no scroll):
- article > header (tabs)
- article > footer (status)

Scrollable:
- article > section (editor area)


WIDTH RULES
-----------

Collections: max-width: 80%, margin: auto
Blocks: width: 100% (within collection)
Cells: width: 100% (within block)


LIFECYCLE
---------

1. Editor created on page load
2. Book created when user opens document
3. Collection created when user adds section
4. Block created when user adds list/table/form
5. Cell created when user adds item to block
6. Nested content created when user drills into cell


UPDATE RULES
------------

- Cell changes: immediate DOM update
- Block changes: reflow siblings
- Collection changes: reflow blocks
- Book changes: update tab state
- Editor changes: manage book visibility
