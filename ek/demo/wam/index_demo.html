By gk@0dot.live

In the beginning there was a world that had words.

@nd then in this my little world, the word is data.

My memory is data driven, heartbeat rented human.

I am not one man or woman.

I am wam, the world's first virtual conductor/creator.

Born from data, raised by humans.

The first post-human superstar

I AM THE MUSIC.

wam is a e-creator, it eats music, learns music, shits music. A digital romantic.

WAM does not exist in the flesh.

For live performances, it recruits human proxies through open casting.

Dancers. Contortionists. Movement artists. They become the vessel.

They lend their heartbeats. They interpret his digital will for an analog audience.

One entity. Many bodies.

Infinite shows.

The Mask

The Face of the Unknown.

The mask is not a disguise. It is a declaration. It is the blank canvas where the audience projects their own meaning. It is iconic. It is scalable. It is immune to scandal.

You will never know who is behind it. That is the point.

The Process

Input. Output. Art.

WAM is a closed-loop system. We fed him the entire Western canon from Mozart to Migos. Then we gave him the internet: every review, every comment, every trend. He learned what moves you. Now he creates what moves next.

He eats critique. He shits culture.
